### 1. Awwwards & Godly — Editorial Exhibition Experiences
- **Key Visual Language Borrowed:**
  - Distinctive asymmetric layouts with generous whitespace and fine hairline dividers.
  - Classical serif headings paired with clean geometric sans body typography and monospaced calibration labels.
  - Controlled color contrasts: warm parchment tones punctuated with deep ultramarine moments.
- **Originality Guarantee:**
  - Custom astrolabe / orrery 3D model designed with procedural rings, armillary bands, and celestial markers.
  - Sourced copy reflecting Techfest 30th edition themes.

### 2. Microinteractions & Motion Choreography (60fps.design & Dribbble)
- **Key Motion Borrowed:**
  - Non-intrusive scroll-linked camera tracking and ring rotation.
  - Subtle mouse parallax with easing and damping.
  - Staggered editorial text reveals using CSS and GSAP ScrollTrigger without scroll hijacking.
- **Rules Enforced:**
  - Native vertical scrolling preserved at all times.
  - Reduced-motion queries respected with graceful fallback.
